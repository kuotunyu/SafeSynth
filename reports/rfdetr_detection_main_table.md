## 6. Caveats

**H4 gate: FAILED, and the results below inherit it.** A logistic probe separates synthetic patches from real ones at AUC **0.9053** (95% CI 0.9013-0.9090, 106,144 patches) against a pre-registered ceiling of 0.60. The synthetic images are trivially distinguishable from real ones, so any gain an arm shows here may be a detectable-domain effect rather than a useful one. ADR-011 allows the 1x pool anyway and forbids 2x; it does not allow calling the gate passed.

**Every claim above is relative.** Roughly two thirds of the real objects in this dataset are unannotated (ADR-003), so an absolute AP measures the annotation as much as the detector. Only arm-versus-arm differences on this same frozen Test split mean anything.
